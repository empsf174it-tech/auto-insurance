What to expect when you claim

The day it matters, you should not have to read fine print. Here is how it works.

Tell us within 48 hours of the incident — ideally straight from the roadside using the form above. Early intimation lets us dispatch a surveyor quickly and keeps your claim clean. For theft or any third-party injury, file a police FIR first and report to us the same day.

Your policy number, the vehicle registration certificate, the driving licence of whoever was driving, and photographs of the damage. Theft and third-party claims also need a copy of the FIR. For reimbursement claims, keep the original repair invoice and payment receipt.

At any of our 5,000+ network garages the repair is cashless — we settle the approved amount directly with the workshop and you pay only your deductible and any non-covered items. If you use a garage outside the network, you pay the bill and we reimburse the approved amount, usually within five working days of receiving your documents.

An own-damage claim resets your no-claim bonus to zero at the next renewal. Small repairs are sometimes cheaper to pay for yourself than to lose an accumulated 35–50% discount, and our claims team will tell you honestly when that is the case. Third-party-only claims and windscreen claims under most add-ons do not affect your NCB.

Still unsure? Talk to a human — our team answers 24/7.
