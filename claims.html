Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to file a claim?

You typically need your policy number, vehicle registration certificate (RC), driving license of the person driving at the time of the incident, and photos of the damage. An FIR copy may be required for major accidents or theft.

How long does claim settlement take?

For cashless claims at our network garages, approval is usually given within 2-4 hours of the surveyor's report. Total settlement time depends on the extent of repairs required.

Can I claim if I don't use a network garage?

Yes, you can use any garage. However, you will need to pay for the repairs upfront and submit the original bills and receipts for reimbursement, which usually takes 7-10 working days to process.
