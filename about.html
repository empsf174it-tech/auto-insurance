Our story

Built by people who hated filing claims

Founded in 2018, Apex Auto was born out of a simple frustration: why does buying insurance and filing a claim feel so complicated? We saw an industry built on confusing jargon, endless paperwork, and slow responses.

So we built a digital-first insurer that puts the customer in the driver's seat. By leaning on technology instead of bureaucracy, we deliver instant quotes, customisable policies, and claim settlements measured in hours.

Today we protect over 2 million vehicles nationwide — from daily commuter bikes to weekend sports cars.
