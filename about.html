Driving Trust Forward

At Apex Auto, we believe insurance shouldn't be a hassle. We're on a mission to make protecting your vehicle as seamless as the ride itself.

Our Story

Founded in 2018, Apex Auto was born out of a simple frustration: why does buying insurance and filing claims feel so complicated? We saw an industry built on confusing jargon, endless paperwork, and slow responses.

We set out to build a digital-first insurance company that puts the customer in the driver's seat. By leveraging technology, we've stripped away the bureaucracy, enabling instant quotes, customized policies, and lightning-fast claim settlements.

Today, we proudly protect over 2 million vehicles nationwide, from daily commuter bikes to luxury sports cars.

Our Mission

To provide transparent, affordable, and accessible auto insurance, ensuring every driver and rider feels secure on their journey.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and customer-centric mobility protection brand, pioneering digital innovations in the insurance space.
