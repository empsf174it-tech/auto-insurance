Key Exclusions

We believe in full transparency. Here is what is not covered under standard policies:

  • Wear and tear aging of the vehicle
  • Electrical or mechanical breakdowns (unless Engine Protect add-on is chosen)
  • Damage occurring outside geographical limits of India
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Driving without a valid driving license
  • Using a private vehicle for commercial purposes

Available Add-ons

Supercharge your policy with these optional covers:
